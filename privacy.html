Privacy

Last updated 9 August 2026

This page covers two separate things: how this website treats visitors, and where to find the privacy policy for each app I publish. Each app has its own policy, and that policy is the one that governs the app.

This website

  • The site is a set of static pages. There are no accounts, no logins, and no forms.
  • It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising services.
  • It loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or trackers — every file comes from this domain.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes standard request data such as your IP address and browser user agent in order to serve the page and protect the service. That processing is covered by GitHub's privacy statement, and I do not receive, store, or have access to those logs.
  • Links marked with an outbound destination — the App Store, GitHub repositories — take you to services run by other companies, with their own privacy practices.

Email you send me

If you email me for support, I receive your message, your email address, and whatever you choose to include. I use it only to answer you, I do not add it to any mailing list, and I do not share it. I keep support correspondence only as long as it is useful for handling the issue, and you may ask me to delete it at any time by writing to chaurasiabhasker@gmail.com.

Please do not attach documents, photos, or identity paperwork to a support email — I do not need them, and they are better left on your own device.

App privacy policies

DocAura — iPhone and iPad

DocAura makes no network requests at all. Scanning, text recognition, and every PDF operation run on your device, and your files stay in the iOS Files app under your control. There is no account, no identifier, no analytics, and no third-party code.

  • Full DocAura privacy policy
  • DocAura website

PhotoPorter — Mac

PhotoPorter copies photos and videos from a connected iPhone or iPad to a folder you choose on your Mac. Your library is not uploaded anywhere, and the record of what has already been imported is kept locally on your own machine.

  • Full PhotoPorter privacy policy
  • PhotoPorter terms of use

Children

My apps are general-purpose tools and are not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this statement changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes to an individual app's policy are published on that app's own policy page.
