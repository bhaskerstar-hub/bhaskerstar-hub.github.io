Support

Last updated 9 August 2026

Every app here is made and supported by one person, and email reaches me directly. Write to chaurasiabhasker@gmail.com and I will normally reply within a few business days.

Before you write

These details make a problem much faster to fix, so include them where they apply:

  • Which app, and the version number shown on its settings or about screen.
  • Your device and OS version — for example "iPhone 15, iOS 18.5" or "MacBook Air M2, macOS 15.4".
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording if the problem is visual.

Please do not send documents, photos, or identity paperwork with a support request. I do not need your files to diagnose a problem, and I would rather they stayed on your device.

DocAura

iPhone and iPad · iOS 16.0 or later · Free

  • DocAura on the App Store
  • Product website and feature list
  • Privacy policy

Common questions: scanned files live in the iOS Files app under DocAura, where you can move, back up, or delete them yourself. The document camera needs a physical device — it is not available in the Simulator. If a printed ID or passport photo comes out the wrong size, check that your printer's scaling is set to 100% rather than "fit to page", since the app lays those sheets out at true physical size.

PhotoPorter

Mac · macOS 14.0 or later · Free

  • PhotoPorter on the Mac App Store
  • Support and legal information
  • Privacy policy
  • Terms of use

Common questions: if your iPhone or iPad does not appear, unlock the device, connect it with a cable, and tap Trust on the prompt that appears on the phone. PhotoPorter keeps a record of what it has already copied, so reconnecting the same device shows you what is new rather than offering the whole camera roll again.

Open-source projects

For the public repositories listed on the home page, please open an issue on the relevant GitHub repository rather than emailing — that keeps the discussion where other users can find it.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security or privacy problem in one of my apps, email chaurasiabhasker@gmail.com with the subject line "Security" and enough detail to reproduce it. Please give me a reasonable chance to fix the issue before publishing it.
